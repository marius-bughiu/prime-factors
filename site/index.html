What the proofs establish

Fermat search

Write n = a² − b². A square hit gives factors a − b and a + b. A gap bound proves which close factor pairs a tested interval can cover.

Read the window theorem

Dirichlet GRH

A bound on small witnesses motivates a modular search when p − 1 divides q − 1. A successful gcd produces a divisor. The analytic witness bound comes from the literature.

Trace the argument

Exact divisor sums

For distinct primes, knowing σ(pq) determines their sum and hence both roots. Robin’s inequality gives an upper bound on σ. The gap between the two limits its use here.
