What a product reveals about its factors

If you multiply two randomly chosen 1,024-bit primes and give us only the result, we have no practical way to find them in general. We have recovered the primes in specially constructed 2,048-bit examples. Here we show which patterns made that possible.

The factor sum

S² − 4N = (q − p)²

Knowing S = p + q determines both primes. The product restricts S differently modulo each small prime.
